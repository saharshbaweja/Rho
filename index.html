    One request, the entire mission

    How a mission runs

    “Audit every streetlight in Back Bay between Clarendon and Fairfield tonight and flag any that are out.”

    Operator intent · Boston demo
    1. 1

      Task

      Aeros reads the request, finds the 83 streetlights in the asset register and pulls the outage history.

    2. 2

      Check

      Airspace, weather, night-ops rules and fleet health are checked, each with its source attached.

    3. 3

      Approve

      The operator reviews the plan and the recommended aircraft and dock before anything flies.

    4. 4

      Report

      Findings are reviewed and the outages go back to the job as a work order.

    The platform

    From tasking to debrief

    • Dispatch

      Turn a sentence, a location or a connected job into missions and coverage areas, with constraints and approvals attached.

    • Fleet

      See aircraft, docks, readiness and scene feeds across every connected provider, and keep reserve capacity in hand.

    • Readiness

      Check weather, airspace, site access and aircraft limits against your procedures. Stale or missing inputs stay visible.

    • Recover

      When a battery runs low or priorities change, compare a relief aircraft, a new route or a delay before approving the next move.

    • Control

      Control room and field teams work from the same scene and brief, with a record of who authorised each change.

    • Report

      Coverage, observations and exceptions return to the workflow that requested the mission.
